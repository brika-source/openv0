Questions

Asked most often.

What does Egyptian law require?

Labour Law No. 12 of 2003 sets duties on medical examination, records and occupational disease reporting. We scope your programme against what actually applies to you.

On-site, or at a clinic?

Mostly on-site. Mobile teams bring portable audiometry, spirometry and phlebotomy to you, which removes travel time and lifts attendance.

Who sees medical results?

Only the physician and the employee. You receive a fitness decision and anonymised trends — never the clinical detail.

How fast can you start?

Two to three weeks for a standard scope. Emergency and standby cover moves faster.

Ergonomics & MSD prevention

The injuries that never reach the incident report

Musculoskeletal disorders do not announce themselves. They accumulate quietly in the tasks nobody has measured — then arrive as absence, claims and lost experience.

Workstation assessment

Measure the task

REBA, RULA and the NIOSH lifting equation, so results are comparable across sites and over time — not an opinion about a complaint.

Rank by cost and effect

Most of the benefit comes from layout, sequence and rotation. Capital items are presented separately, for your own business case.

Prove it held

We baseline absence days, discomfort scores and claim counts before the intervention, then re-measure. The comparison is the report.

Mental health & employee support

How we work

Assess before you buy anything

Five stages. The last one is the stage most providers leave out — and the only one that tells you whether the first four were worth paying for.

STAGE 01

Assess

We walk the site, read your hazard register and incident history, and establish what your exposures actually are.

STAGE 02

Design

A written programme scoped to those exposures: protocols, intervals, roles covered, and a cost you can budget against.

STAGE 03

Deliver

Examinations and training on a fixed calendar, mostly on-site, scheduled around your shift pattern.

STAGE 04

Report

Fitness decisions to managers, anonymised trends to HSE and HR, audit-ready records to your retention schedule.

STAGE 05

Review

Scheduled review against the baseline. If a programme is not moving its metric, that shows up here.
